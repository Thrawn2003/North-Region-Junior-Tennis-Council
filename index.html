About the Council

Supporting the tennis community, and the community beyond it.

We are a charitable organisation, and tennis is how we serve. The Council exists to support the people who make the sport possible: families, clubs, coaches and volunteers. We also work to make sure no young person in the Northern Region is left on the sideline because of cost or circumstance.

How we give back

Programs built around people, not just performance.

Access

Tennis for Everyone

Free and subsidised places so cost is never the reason a child misses out on the game.

Fundraising

Charity Tournaments

Community tournaments and fun days where every entry helps local causes and families in need.

Equipment

Racquet & Gear Drive

Donated racquets, balls and clothing collected, refreshed and passed on to young players.

Mentoring

Volunteer & Mentor Network

Older juniors and club volunteers guiding younger players, and growing the next generation of givers.
