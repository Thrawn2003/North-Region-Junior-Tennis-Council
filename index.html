About the Council

Built on competition, character and community.

We are the governing voice for junior tennis across the Northern Region. From a child's first rally to a first tournament title, the Council provides the structure, standards and support that turn potential into performance.

  • 01

    Excellence

    Rigorous standards in coaching, officiating and competition.

  • 02

    Integrity

    Fair play, respect for opponents and respect for the game.

  • 03

    Opportunity

    A clear pathway for every junior, at every level.

Pathways

A program for every stage of the journey.

Ages 5–8

Rally Starters

Modified courts and low-compression balls make a joyful first step into the sport.

Ages 9–12

Rising Stars

Structured group coaching and friendly match play to build technique and confidence.

Ages 13–15

Regional Squad

Selection-based training with strength & conditioning, tactics and sports psychology.

Ages 16–18

Elite Performance

Tournament pathways, college and state-level exposure, and mentorship from top coaches.
