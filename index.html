Tennis that gives back

We're the Northern Region Tennis Junior Council, a charity for the tennis community. We back local clubs and the volunteers who keep them running, and we want every young person in the region who wants to play to be able to.

Get involved About us

Community first

Lots of tennis organisations exist to produce better players. We're happy when that happens, but it isn't why we're here. We're here for the people around the game: families, clubs, coaches and volunteers.

Most of what we do is charity work. If a family needs help getting a child onto the court, or a club needs support, that's where our time and money go.
