About us

A charity built around tennis and the people who play it.

Who we are

The Northern Region Tennis Junior Council is a charity that looks after the tennis community in our region. We support the families, clubs, coaches and volunteers who make the game happen, and we try to make sure no young person misses out on playing.

We aren't a performance academy. If our juniors turn into strong players, that's great. What we really want is a tennis community where people look out for each other.
