About us

A charity built on the love of tennis.

We exist to support the tennis community, and to use the game to do good in the communities around it.

Our purpose

The Northern Region Tennis Junior Council is a charitable organisation. Tennis is how we serve: we support the families, clubs, coaches and volunteers who make the sport possible, and we work to make sure no young person in the Northern Region is left on the sideline.

Our focus is the community first. Building strong players is a welcome result, but our aim is a generous, inclusive and connected tennis family that gives back.
